# Venue and claims plan

## Primary target

**[Blockchain: Research and Applications (BCRA)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/blockchain-research-and-applications)** is the best thematic target. It publishes empirical
blockchain security/detection work, including the 2025 Uniswap V3 transaction-window study and the
2026 meme-coin rug-pull study that are direct neighbors of this work. The project is stronger as a
blockchain measurement/evaluation paper than as a new-algorithm paper.

At submission time, re-check the current Guide for Authors, article type, APC/open-access terms and
the institution's live Scopus quartile. Journal metrics and quartiles change and must not be frozen
from a web snippet. **Computers & Security** is a possible backup if the security/evaluation argument
is made sharper. **Expert Systems with Applications** is not the first choice unless the work gains
substantial model novelty.

## Allowed headline claims

- A fixed-landmark, censored and purged chronological protocol for early Uniswap V2 rug-like
  liquidity-collapse prediction.
- A controlled empirical estimate of random-split and label-dependent-window optimism.
- An operational trade-off between longer observation, eligible cohort size and end-to-end coverage.
- Robustness across common cohorts, rolling temporal folds, cold starts, label rules and feature groups.

## Claims to avoid

- “First early rug-pull detector,” “first leakage-resistant dataset,” or universal state of the art.
- Direct numerical superiority over papers with different labels, chains, periods or cohorts.
- Calling every mechanically detected liquidity collapse intentional fraud.
- Saying the Setup A software artifact is an IMCOM paper or assigning it an unverified DOI.
- Calling an AI-assisted audit two independent human reviews.
